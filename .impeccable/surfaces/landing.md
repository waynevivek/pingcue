# Surface Brief: Landing Page (`app/page.tsx`)

- **Scope:** Public landing page (`/`)
- **Mode:** Persuade
- **Audience & Job:** Solo freelancers (India and global, frequently browsing on a phone) who send payment links by email or WhatsApp and want unpaid invoices chased politely without adopting an accounting suite.
- **Primary Action:** Buy the **$19 one-time Founding Pass** (secondary path: start free with 1 active invoice at `/login`).
- **Proof & Constraints:** Demonstrate the escalating schedule (`-3d`, `0d`, `+3d`, `+7d`, `+14d`, `+30d`), `Reply-To` email copy shift, 1-click Pause, test email preview, and `wa.me` WhatsApp nudge using clearly synthetic sample data. Zero stock photos, zero purple gradients, zero emoji in labels, zero fabricated testimonials or metrics. Built with Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and `shadcn/ui`.
- **Unresolved Decisions:** Exact copy layout and interactive cadence preview state transitions to be finalized during implementation.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** PingCue is a quiet dispatch passbook for link-based freelancers—replacing bloated accounting-suite pitch pages and generic SaaS gradient heroes with an immediate, working proof of how polite reminders escalate to firm notice without hurting client relationships.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Mineral Paper (`#f6f7f4`) ground, Crisp Sheet (`#ffffff`) ledger cards with 1px hairlines, Deep Pine Slate (`#16382f`) structural ink and `$19` Founding Pass CTA, Brass Ochre (`#b47818`) for Firm escalation stamps, Crimson (`#b91c1c`) strictly for Overdue/Final, set in `Bricolage Grotesque`, `Figtree`, and `JetBrains Mono` (`tabular-nums`) on `shadcn/ui` primitives.
- **STORY:** Visitor recognizes immediately that PingCue works with the payment links they already send; sees the `-3d` to `+30d` tone progression, partial-balance math, 1-click Pause, and WhatsApp nudge; trusts the calm, transparent `$0` (1 invoice) vs. `$19` one-time pricing; and clicks to buy the Founding Pass.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Top nav with PingCue wordmark, anchor links, Sign in, and compact `$19` Founding Pass CTA; headline, concise offer copy, primary `$19` Founding Pass button, and secondary anchor above or beside the interactive **Escalation Cadence Rail** preview showing a synthetic invoice (`INR`, `Partial` balance, and stepped dispatch stamps).
- **FORM:** Code-first implementation of the **Passbook & Dispatch** seed direction; position 1 of 4 user-reviewed directions; seed: none (unavailable).
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
